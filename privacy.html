Privacy Notice

Version 2026-10-13 · Applies to the Markado website and app

Markado is a tool for teachers to record, import, publish and print learners' grades. This notice explains what personal information Markado handles, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and what your rights are under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). We try to keep it plain; if anything is unclear, write to us at markado.work@gmail.com.

1. Who Is Responsible For What

  • Teachers' account information (name, email, subscription): Markado ("we", "us") decides how it is used, so we are the personal information controller for it.
  • Learners' information (names, LRNs, grades and so on): the teacher, on behalf of their school, decides which learners to enter and why. Markado only stores and handles that information on the teacher's instructions, as a personal information processor. Questions about a learner's record should go first to the teacher or school.

2. What We Collect

From teachers

  • Full name and email address, and a username or mobile number if one is provided.
  • Your password. We never store it as typed; we keep only a salted one-way hash of it.
  • School details you enter on the Home page: school name, region, division, district, municipality, principal's name, class days and school logo.
  • Subscription records: trial dates, plan, payment reference number and any message you send with a plan request.
  • Account activity records, such as when you registered, logged in, published grades, downloaded your data, or agreed to this notice and the Terms of Use (and which version).

About learners (entered or imported by the teacher)

  • Full name, sex, Learner Reference Number (LRN) and birthdate where provided.
  • Class (grade level, section, school year), subjects, and term grades with their publication status.
  • A learner PIN, used so a learner can see their own published grades. It is stored only as a one-way hash.

An E-CR file you import is read to take out names, class details and term grades; the file itself is not kept.

3. Why We Use It

  • To run the service: sign you in, keep your classes and grades, let you publish grades, and let learners see their own published grades.
  • To confirm your email address and to send account emails (confirmation codes, password resets).
  • To manage trials, plans and payments, and to prevent a free trial being used again and again.
  • To keep the service secure, investigate problems and give support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use it to profile anyone.

4. Who Can See It

  • You see only your own classes and learners.
  • A learner sees only their own grades, and only after the teacher publishes them.
  • Markado's administrator can see account and subscription details and may open account data when needed for support, security or legal reasons.
  • Service providers that host the service: Google (Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Apps Script store the data and send our emails) and the web host that serves the Markado pages. They handle data only to provide those services.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

5. Where It Is Stored

Data is stored in Google's cloud. Google's servers may be outside the Philippines, so your information may be processed in other countries. Each teacher's classes, learners and grades are kept in a separate spreadsheet that is not shared with other teachers.

6. What Is Kept On Your Device

So Markado can work without internet, the app keeps on your device (in the browser's storage) a copy of your classes, learners, subjects, grades and report cards, any grade changes still waiting to be sent, a copy of your school logo, your login session, your light or dark mode choice, and (on a learner's device) the time grades were last viewed, so new grades can be marked, and the app's own files. Logging out removes the saved copy of your data; changes not yet sent are kept so they are not lost and are sent the next time you log in. On a shared or public computer, always log out. If you tick Remember me when you log in, your login stays on that device for up to 30 days; otherwise it ends when you close the tab, or after 12 hours. Changing your password signs you out everywhere else. Markado does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

7. How Long We Keep It

  • Your account and class data are kept while your account exists. You can delete classes at any time, and you can delete your whole account (see section 9).
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from the app straight away and your spreadsheet is moved to Google Drive's trash, which Google empties after about 30 days.
  • Some small records remain after deletion: the email and dates of a free trial (so a trial is not repeated), subscription and payment-request records (they carry no name or email), and account-activity entries with their details removed. Payment records are kept for as long as accounting and tax rules require.
  • When a teacher promotes a class at the end of the school year, the old class is archived: it is kept, read-only, so that grades and reports stay available. Archived classes are not deleted automatically. Teachers are encouraged to delete the classes of past school years when they no longer need them.

8. How We Protect It

Pages and data travel over HTTPS. Passwords and learner PINs are stored only as salted hashes. Login sessions expire. Each teacher's data sits in its own spreadsheet with sharing restricted, and access by our administrator is limited to what is needed. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it private. If a personal data breach happens that must be reported, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected as the law requires.

9. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your personal data, to object, to correct it, to ask for it to be blocked or removed, to data portability, to claim compensation for damages, and to complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

  • Teachers: in the app, go to Settings, then Privacy and Your Data. Download My Data gives you a file with everything stored in your account. Delete My Account permanently deletes your account and data. You can correct your classes and learners in the app, and change your password under My Account.
  • Learners and parents or guardians: please ask your teacher or school first, since they decide what is recorded. You may also write to us.
  • For anything else, email markado.work@gmail.com. We may need to confirm who you are before acting on a request.

10. Learners and Children

Most learners are minors. Teachers must only enter learners they are authorized by their school to record, must follow their school's and DepEd's rules on learner information, and must enter only what they need. Markado does not contact learners or use their information for any other purpose.

11. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will update the version above and tell registered teachers.
